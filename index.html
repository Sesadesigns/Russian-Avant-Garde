Russian Avant Garde Library

Between 1890 and 1930, a group of radical artists in the Russian Empire and early Soviet Union set out to reinvent the visual world from scratch. They called into question everything — form, colour, space, and the very purpose of art itself — and in doing so produced one of the most electrifying movements in modern history.

This library is a living archive of the artists who gave those ideas form. Browse the collection, filter by discipline, and trace the movements — Futurism, Suprematism, Constructivism, Rayonism, Cubo-Futurism, Theatre, and Abstract — through the biographies and works of nineteen artists who defined the era.

For a deeper look at the collection, visit the Library Overview — an interactive breakdown of the archive by movement, discipline, decade, gender, and country of birth, with percentages to map the shape of the collection as a whole.
